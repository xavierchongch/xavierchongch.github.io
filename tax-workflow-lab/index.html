The playbook

Reliable by design.

Automation is useful only when it improves the operating model. These are the three patterns I reach for first.

Structure before speed

Fix the inputs before automating the output.

Most fragile workpapers are data-model problems wearing spreadsheet clothes. Define the entity, period, source and tax treatment once, then let each downstream schedule reuse that structure.

DefineOne clear grain for every record.
MapBusiness rules kept outside the raw data.
ReuseOne prepared dataset, many controlled outputs.
Controls in the flow

Make exceptions visible where they happen.

A good workflow does not merely calculate. It proves completeness, shows what changed, and tells the reviewer exactly where attention is needed.

ReconcileTie source totals before transformation.
FlagRoute unusual items to human review.
EvidenceKeep the support beside the conclusion.
Design for handoff

Build something the next person can trust.

The true test of a process is whether someone else can operate, review and troubleshoot it without reconstructing the creator's memory.

OrientMake status and ownership obvious.
ExplainWrite rules in the language of the work.
RecoverDesign graceful paths for failed inputs.
